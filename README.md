# 📊 Excel Sales & Profitability Dashboard

An interactive **Microsoft Excel dashboard** designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, regional performance, product trends, targets, and returns using transactional sales data.

The project demonstrates how raw business data can be transformed into meaningful **KPIs, visualizations, and actionable business insights** using Excel.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project analyzes **500 orders** recorded between **January 2024 and December 2025**.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance and allows users to explore key metrics across different dimensions such as:

* Region
* City
* Category
* Sub-Category
* Department
* Customer Type
* Order Priority
* Month & Year

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Monitor overall sales and profitability
* Identify high-performing regions and cities
* Analyze category and sub-category performance
* Compare new and returning customers
* Track sales targets and achievement
* Evaluate profit margins and expenses
* Analyze product returns
* Identify monthly sales trends
* Discover opportunities for business improvement

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🌎 Regional Performance

The **West region** generated the highest revenue, contributing approximately **$130.43K**.

### 💻 Category Performance

**Technology** was the top-performing category with approximately **$104.42K** in revenue.

### 📱 Product Performance

**Phones** were the leading sub-category, generating approximately **$77.88K** in revenue.

### 👥 Customer Performance

Returning customers generated approximately **$247.36K** in revenue, highlighting the significant contribution of repeat customers.

### 🏙️ City Performance

**New York** was the highest-revenue city, generating approximately **$97.01K**.

### 📅 Monthly Trend

**November 2025** recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately **$29.59K**.

### 🔄 Returns

The overall return rate was approximately **4.8%**, based on 2,103 units sold.
